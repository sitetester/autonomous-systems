# Timing & Rate Considerations

Everything upstream (preprocessing, YOLO inference, NMS) takes real time to run, and that time distribution determines how fast the drone can actually react to what it sees.

- **The Chain of Delays:** Each stage adds latency (delay) — frame acquisition, preprocessing (resize/convert/normalize), YOLO inference itself, then NMS cleanup. The total time from "camera captures a frame" to "Detection2DArray published" is the sum of all of these, not just the inference step alone.
- **Why Inference Dominates:** Preprocessing and NMS are comparatively cheap (simple math, small arrays). YOLO inference is the expensive part — it's a full forward pass through a neural network — so it's usually the bottleneck that determines overall pipeline latency.
- **The Real Constraint — Detection Rate vs. Camera Frame Rate:** The camera might capture frames at, say, 30 fps, but if inference takes longer than 1/30th of a second per frame on the companion computer's hardware, the detection node can't keep up. In practice, the node either drops frames (skips ones it doesn't have time to process) or falls behind and publishes stale detections.
- **Why This Matters for Flight Safety:** A drone moving at speed covers real distance between one detection and the next. If detection rate drops too low, the gap between "obstacle appears in frame" and "obstacle appears in /detections" grows — meaning the path planner is reacting to where an obstacle *was*, not where it *is* now. This is exactly why the earlier optimization step (TensorRT/ONNX conversion) matters: it's not just about efficiency, it's what keeps detection rate high enough to be safe at flight speed.
- **Companion Computer Hardware Sets the Ceiling:** A Jetson's onboard GPU can deliver meaningfully higher detection rates than a Raspberry Pi doing inference on CPU (or a small USB accelerator) — this is the practical reason hardware choice on the companion computer directly determines how fast and safely the drone can fly through obstacle-dense environments.
